# Idiotinator
Local-first assistant based on PocketPal AI (MIT). Preserve upstream attribution and polished components.

## 0.1.5
ARM64-first Poco F5 development, original workspace, hardware monitoring, model recommendations, stable/advanced/experimental profiles, independent model storage. Keep x86_64 optional for emulator tests.

## Stage 2
Persistent model-independent memory, adaptive learning from verified corrections, editable/exportable knowledge, web research and authorized phone tools.

## Stage 3
Adaptive Council: single-model reflection, sequential and optional parallel models, tool verification, compare accuracy and cost against single model. Candidate models Qwen3.5 4B/9B, Gemma 4 E4B, LFM3 1.7B, subject to compatibility and benchmarks. Document RAG.

## Stage 4
Vision, OCR, scanned PDFs, offline speech and voice interaction.

## Stage 5
Thermal/resource safeguards, broad device compatibility, experimental LoRA with evaluation and rollback.
