# Stabilization roadmap

1. Complete PocketPal comparative audit and downloader reliability package.
2. Verify hardware detection, inference and fallback.
3. Repair and verify voice and vision.
4. Correct UI and assistant behavior without redesigning during stabilization.
5. Validate intelligence and adaptive offline memory.
6. Run automated regression and compilation gates.
7. Produce one verified release-candidate APK.
8. Physical-device testing and stable v0.2.0 approval.

This is the recovered high-level order, not a replacement for any existing detailed engineering plan.
